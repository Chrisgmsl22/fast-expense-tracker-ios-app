Privacy policyNew · Brief 11
Settings
Privacy
Updated November 2026Copy needs owner sign-off
What is stored
Your name, email and password (hashed). The money you log: expenses, income, cards, categories, limits and settlement entries, including your partner's name.
Where
In a Postgres database hosted by Neon. The app and its API run on Vercel.
Who can read it
You. The app's owner, as database admin, can also read it, and only does so to fix a problem.
What error reports carry
When something breaks, a report with the screen, the time and technical details goes to the owner. [Owner to confirm which fields are scrubbed.]
How to ask for deletion
Delete your account in Settings › Account, or message the owner and your data is removed.
