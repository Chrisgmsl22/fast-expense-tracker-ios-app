## Description

Fast Expense is a private expense tracker for you and your partner. Log spending in seconds, always see your real share, and stay on your plan.

SEE WHAT THINGS REALLY COST YOU

Split a dinner 68/32 and only your 68% counts toward your budget. The full charge still shows on the card, so your card balance stays right.

STAY ON YOUR PLAN

Your income splits into Essentials, Discretionary and Savings: 50/25/25 by default, or your own split. Every category has its own limit and colour.

KNOW WHO OWES WHO

One running balance with your partner. Log what they paid you, what you owe them, and settle up in two taps.

EVERY CARD, ONE CLEAR BALANCE

Track what you charged, paid and redeemed with points on each card. A card payment is never counted as spending.

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT

One tap hides every amount on screen. No ads and no tracking.

Fast Expense works in Mexican pesos, in English and Spanish.
